This is an unofficial NeoForge 26.1.2 port of The Twilight Forest by TeamTwilight (Benimatic, AtomicBlom, Drullkus, Killer_Demon, quadraxis, Tamaized, williewillus, Andromander, GizmoTheMoonPig, jodlodi, SleepyHorse); 26.1 port by Lonmo0208. The original authors did not make it and do not support it, so please report problems with this build at https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/issues and not to them.

What changed

Built unmodified from Lonmo0208's community 26.1 port (Twilightforest-Re26).
