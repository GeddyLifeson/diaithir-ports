This is an unofficial NeoForge 26.1.2 port of Flux Networks by SonarSonic and BloCamLimb. The original authors did not make it and do not support it, so please report problems with this build at https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/issues and not to them.

What changed

Ported from the official 1.21 branch. One upstream bug fixed (a mouse drag passed the mouse Y as the drag delta). Left out: the ModernUI screens (the classic GUI is kept), The One Probe and GregTech CEu integrations, the energy bar drawn on storage items in hand, and the 3D animation on the JEI "creating flux" page.
