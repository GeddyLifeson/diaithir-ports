This is an unofficial NeoForge 26.1.2 port of Modern Industrialization by Azercoco and Technici4n. The original authors did not make it and do not support it, so please report problems with this build at https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/issues and not to them.

What changed

Built from Modern Industrialization's own port/26.1 branch with its KubeJS integration switched back on.
