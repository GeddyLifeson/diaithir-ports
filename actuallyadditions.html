This is an unofficial NeoForge 26.1.2 port of Actually Additions by Ellpeck, Mrbysco and contributors. The original authors did not make it and do not support it, so please report problems with this build at https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/issues and not to them.

What changed

Ported from 1.21.1. All nine machine recipes on the 26.1 Recipe API and shown in JEI; energy, fluids and items on NeoForge's transactional transfer API. Left out: the Patchouli manual (no 26.1 Patchouli), the engineer villager and its house, contributor cosmetics (they downloaded a player list), the drill's 3x3/5x5 break outline, and the worm's snail easter egg.
