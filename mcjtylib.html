This is an unofficial NeoForge 26.1.2 port of McJtyLib by McJty. The original authors did not make it and do not support it, so please report problems with this build at https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/issues and not to them.

What changed

Ported from the 1.21_neo branch. Old-style renderers are recorded and replayed through 26.1's render submission, so RFTools kept its rendering code. Left out: The One Probe and Patchouli support, and the multipart, multiblock, spline and font code that RFTools does not use.
