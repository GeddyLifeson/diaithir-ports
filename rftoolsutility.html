This is an unofficial NeoForge 26.1.2 port of RFTools Utility by McJty. The original authors did not make it and do not support it, so please report problems with this build at https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/issues and not to them.

What changed

Ported from the 1.21_neo branch. Left out: the TrueType font option on screens, the tank's fill-level model, item texture switches (syringe fill, porter charge, linked power card), and the 3D screen preview in the tablet GUI.
