# Test results

Build: `./gradlew build` (JDK 25, NeoForge 26.1.2.116). Boot: `gradle runServer` on a flat world, rcon, no players.

- Server boots with no ERROR lines from the mod (the only ERROR is the pack-less flat generator "No key layers", unrelated). 29 profiles and 12 elite bases loaded; worldgen JSON (10 structures, template pools, structure sets, biome tags) passes registry load.
- `/bossesselftest`: bosses tested=29 skipped=0 ok=1455 bad=0. Per boss, with a fake player on a stone platform: spawns, controller attached, bar shows to the fighter, no-despawn flag, every attack of its profile telegraphs then fires, every phase change (tag persisted, shield during the change), arena pushes a fighter who leaves, Age-gated loot (Age I roll never larger than Age VII), trophy drop, kill runs the death hook and drops items, effective health grows with the Age (health above the 1024 attribute ceiling is paid by dividing damage). Also: attach path (a profile adopts another mod's creature, here a vanilla husk standing in for diaithir_myth entities), the altar (sleeps below its Age, wakes once, remembers a defeat, rests per respawn rule), and elites (zombie, skeleton, creeper get 3 affixes at Age VII, hulking and shielded numbers, roll rate about the table).
- rcon: `summon diaithirbosses:<id>` for all 29 ids (29 summoned), `/boss spawnall` (29), `/boss list`, `/place structure diaithirbosses:tribal_chief` on a normal world generates it, `/place template` gives an altar block entity with the right boss id.
- Not tested: a real client (the vanilla-model renderers with generated textures were not looked at), diaithir_myth entities themselves (no jar in the pack build yet; the attach path is tested with a husk), real multiplayer balance, natural elite spawning over time.
