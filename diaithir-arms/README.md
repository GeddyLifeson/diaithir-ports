# Diaithir Arms (MIT, clean-room)

Original armour and weapons for every Age of Diaithir. Nothing here is ported or copied: code is written against the 26.1 item component API, art is generated by `tools/gen.py` from the vanilla sprites (luminance ramps, worn edges, grain, scratches, per-material detail: bone stitching, copper verdigris, bronze with red and blue enamel studs, mail rings and rust, brushed steel with rivets, webbing grid, carbon twill with ceramic tiles, teal and gold bands, cold lattice lines).

| Age | Armour (helmet, chest, legs, boots) | Weapons and gear |
|---|---|---|
| I | bone-plated hide | hunter's bow, shield, bone plate |
| II | copper | bow, shield |
| III | bronze | bow, shield |
| IV | iron mail | bow, crossbow, shield |
| V | steel plate | sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, shield |
| VI | ballistic weave over a steel pot | tempered sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, riot shield; ballistic weave, tempered ingot |
| VII | ceramic and carbon composite | composite sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, shield; ceramic plate |
| Rodos | Rodos alloy | sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, shield; Rodos alloy ingot |
| Future | star-lattice | same set; star lattice |

75 gear items and 6 crafting parts. Wood, stone, copper, bronze and iron melee weapons are the Spartan Weaponry port's job; this mod covers armour and shields for all Ages, bows and crossbows for all Ages, and melee from Age V on.

Armour uses `ArmorMaterial` plus equipment assets (`assets/diaithirarms/equipment/*.json`, humanoid, leggings and baby layers). Swords, axes and spears use `ToolMaterial` (`sword`, `axe`, `spear` item properties, spear gets the kinetic and piercing components). Bows and crossbows are `ArmsBow` and `ArmsCrossbow` (arrow damage multiplier per tier). Shields carry the `blocks_attacks` component (block percentage rises per tier) and sit in the off hand.

Recipes use tags: `c:ingots/copper`, `c:ingots/bronze`, `c:ingots/iron`, `c:ingots/steel` (bronze and steel come from other mods in the pack), plus the mod's own parts for Ages I, VI, VII, Rodos and the Future. Repair tags are `diaithirarms:repair/<tier>`.

Names are Diaithiris (`names_map.json` has the English). Ages are in `age_gates.json` (the Future follows Rodos and has no Age key of its own yet, so it shares RODOS). Library entries (gear plus per-Age combat mobs) are `library/arms_library.json` (copied to library-sync/arms.json); `codex/arms_handbook.json` is the chapter source for the Handbooks codex builder.

`/armsselftest` builds every item stack and checks its components. `tools/gen.py <vanilla assets dir>` regenerates everything under `src/main/resources` plus `library/gear.json`, `codex/` and the two json files; `tools/library_mobs.py <mods dir> <nemesis.json> <out>` writes the mob entries and the merged Library file.

## Transmog table (clean-room replacement for Iron's Lib's transmog table)
`diaithirarms:transmog_table` (Age III, 2 string + 4 planks). Put two armour pieces of the same slot in a crafting grid (works in any grid; the table is the themed place): the first in reading order keeps stats, enchantments and durability and takes the second's worn model and inventory icon. Craft a piece with its own original look to undo it. `diaithirarms:transmog` is the special recipe. Selftest: `/armsselftest` ok=78 bad=0 (75 gear + 3 transmog checks). Not tested: client rendering of a transmogged piece, trims on a transmogged piece. Table texture is generated here; Diaithiris block name pending.

## Armour pack for Immersive Vehicles (clean-room replacement for P:WIC)
This jar also carries an Immersive Vehicles content pack, `assets/diaithirtanks/` (any jar with `assets/<id>/packdefinition.json` in the mods folder is loaded by IV, so no extra jar). Everything is original: `tools/gen_tank.py` builds the OBJ models (boxes, prisms, cylinders), the camouflage textures (forest, desert, winter) and all JSON; `tools/preview_tank.py` renders a software preview (`docs/tank_mk1_preview.png`).
Done: two original tank designs (Mk I with a boxy turret, Mk II low and domed), three marks each (6 vehicles) in forest, desert and winter paint (18 vehicle items), two track parts, diesel engine, crew seat, three guns (105, 120, 125 mm autoloader), armour-piercing and high-explosive shells. IV parses every def without errors and registers all 27 items, checked headless (`summon item ... mts:diaithirtanks.<id>`). Diaithiris names (`names_map_tanks.json`), Age gates (`age_gates_tanks.json`, VII and Rodos), library (`library/tanks_library.json`) and Codex handbook (`codex/tanks_handbook.json`) come from `tools/gen_tank_meta.py`; previews in `docs/`.
Not done: driving, turret turn and firing are untested (no headless spawn harness; the first full-pack client join checks them); tracks are static (no tread path or wheel animation); no instruments; the vehicles are original designs, not copies of the P:WIC set (about 20 variants across an American-pattern and a Soviet-pattern family), so old `mts:pwic_*` ids map to the nearest mark in `id_migrations_tanks.json`.

## Haulage and construction pack for Immersive Vehicles (`diaithirtrucks`, clean-room)
Second content pack in this jar (no new jar). No 26.1 mod covers semi trucks, trailers or construction machines (Modrinth check 11-03: the "excavator" hits are digging tools, concrete-mixer-lite is a block), so this builds on Immersive Vehicles like the tank pack. `tools/gen_trucks.py` writes the OBJ models, textures and vehicle/part JSON, `tools/gen_trucks_meta.py` the roster CSV, names, Age gates, library, Codex, yard JSON and part catalog; data is in `tools/truck_roster.py`; `tools/ivgeom.py` holds the geometry helpers shared with `gen_tank.py`.
- **Primitive predecessors** (Ages II to VI, explicit Age in `truck_roster.py`): ox team and horse team on a limber, steam traction engine, Sentinel steam waggon; handcart, two-wheeled cart, ox cart, four-wheel wagon, Conestoga wagon, dray; hand-hauled stone roller, horse roller, tip cart, Fresno scraper, horse grader, steam shovel. They hitch by `drawbar` groups (modern tractors carry a drawbar too). Draft engines burn hay, steam engines coal, using the fuel ids of ports #190 (`diaithir-units` Fuels); IV needs those fuel names mapped (untested). A hand rammer is a tool, not a vehicle, so it is not here.
- **Roster** (`roster/TRUCK_ROSTER.csv`, one Wikipedia page per row, year, Age): 32 modern tractor units (one iconic version per design, Scammell Scarab 1933 to Kenworth T680 2012: conventional, cab-over and three-wheel layouts), 14 semi-trailers (dry van, flatbed, step-deck, lowboy, reefer, tank, car carrier, log, curtainsider, dump, livestock, container chassis, modular heavy-haul, tank-transporter semitrailer) and 13 construction machines (road rollers, transit mixer, concrete pump truck, asphalt paver, road miller, grader, dozer, excavator, wheel loader, backhoe, skid steer, haul truck). Each has three paints. Ages: before 1861 V, to 1945 VI, after VII (`age_gates_trucks.json`).
- **Hitching**: every tractor has a `fifthwheel` hitch group, every semi-trailer a `fifthwheel` hookup group (IV connection groups), so any tractor takes any trailer. Car carrier, lowboy and tank-transporter add mounted `tow` slots on the deck for vehicles.
- **Cargo**: trailers carry real inventory through IV parts: cargo holds of 18, 54 or 108 slots (dry van, reefer, curtainsider, chassis, livestock, flatbed, log, dump, modular) and tanks of 10, 20 or 30 kL (tank trailer, 30 kL; concrete pump 10 kL); the mixer and haul truck carry a hold. `truck_manifest.json` lists payload kg, slots and litres per vehicle for the company loop (cargo demand, depots). The depot link itself is not built: it needs the courier-depot and loader API.
- **Units and upgrades**: power in W (roster kW), mass kg, fuel L, 0.25 L of diesel per kWh at full load. Six engine classes (130 kW to 2.5 MW), each in three tiers per the upgrade rule: stock, efficiency (same W, 15 % less fuel) and speed (+15 % W, +30 % fuel); the engine is the swappable module, the upgrade planner target rule is still to do.
- **Assembly**: `data/diaithirtrucks/yards/<vehicle>.json` and `parts_catalog.json` follow the Assembly Yard spec v1 (ports PR 186, `plans/ASSEMBLY_API.md`): engine, wheels or tracks, seat and cargo stages with SI stats, heavy flag at 500 kg. Frame, cab and body parts have no items yet, so the stages stop at running gear, crew and cargo; the `RolloutHook` waits for the yard core. Until then IV's own vehicle bench crafts them from `materialLists`.
- **Not done**: no animation (wheels spin through IV, tipper, mixer drum, boom and blade are static), fifth-wheel coupling and mounted cars untested in a client, road ties for the construction machines (roller, paver, miller, mixer, grader carry a `road_role` field for the Cars and fuel thread's road system), Diaithiris names are provisional kennings from the archaic layer, workshop-bench blueprints (the benches in ports #180 build held items, not vehicles), Codex quests.
- **Check**: `tools/IvPackCheck.java` loads every def through IV's own `JSONParser` (needs the IV jar and gson on the classpath, JDK 21 is enough): `ok=114 bad=0`, plus model and texture existence. A server boot with the pack is the next step.

## Roster (survey part 2, Spartan Weaponry behaviour, clean-room)
`Roster.java` + `tools/gen_roster.py` add dagger, rapier, katana, glaive, halberd and warhammer in steel, tempered, composite, rodos and future (30 items). Long shapes add entity interaction range; textures are drawn procedurally from each material's sword palette. Age gates follow the material's sword.

## Gadgets (survey part 2: turrets and mining laser, clean-room)
`net.diaithir.arms.gadgets`: `sentry_turret` block (shoots the nearest hostile in line of sight within 14 blocks every 12 ticks, ammo as a block-state level loaded with arrows, no block entity) and `mining_laser` item (cuts the block in the crosshair up to 16 blocks, hardness cap 10, 600 uses). Written from the behaviour of Jungdab's Turrets and Mining Gadgets; no upstream code.
