# Diaithir Arms (MIT, clean-room)

Original armour and weapons for every Age of Diaithir. Nothing here is ported or copied: code is written against the 26.1 item component API, art is generated by `tools/gen.py` from the vanilla sprites (luminance ramps, worn edges, grain, scratches, per-material detail: bone stitching, copper verdigris, bronze with red and blue enamel studs, mail rings and rust, brushed steel with rivets, webbing grid, carbon twill with ceramic tiles, teal and gold bands, cold lattice lines).

| Age | Armour (helmet, chest, legs, boots) | Weapons and gear |
|---|---|---|
| I | bone-plated hide | hunter's bow, shield, bone plate |
| II | copper | bow, shield |
| III | bronze | bow, shield |
| IV | iron mail | bow, crossbow, shield |
| V | steel plate | sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, shield |
| VI | ballistic weave over a steel pot | tempered sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, riot shield; ballistic weave, tempered ingot |
| VII | ceramic and carbon composite | composite sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, shield; ceramic plate |
| Rodos | Rodos alloy | sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, shield; Rodos alloy ingot |
| Future | star-lattice | same set; star lattice |

75 gear items and 6 crafting parts. Wood, stone, copper, bronze and iron melee weapons are the Spartan Weaponry port's job; this mod covers armour and shields for all Ages, bows and crossbows for all Ages, and melee from Age V on.

Armour uses `ArmorMaterial` plus equipment assets (`assets/diaithirarms/equipment/*.json`, humanoid, leggings and baby layers). Swords, axes and spears use `ToolMaterial` (`sword`, `axe`, `spear` item properties, spear gets the kinetic and piercing components). Bows and crossbows are `ArmsBow` and `ArmsCrossbow` (arrow damage multiplier per tier). Shields carry the `blocks_attacks` component (block percentage rises per tier) and sit in the off hand.

Recipes use tags: `c:ingots/copper`, `c:ingots/bronze`, `c:ingots/iron`, `c:ingots/steel` (bronze and steel come from other mods in the pack), plus the mod's own parts for Ages I, VI, VII, Rodos and the Future. Repair tags are `diaithirarms:repair/<tier>`.

Names are Diaithiris (`names_map.json` has the English). Ages are in `age_gates.json` (the Future follows Rodos and has no Age key of its own yet, so it shares RODOS). Library entries (gear plus per-Age combat mobs) are `library/arms_library.json` (copied to library-sync/arms.json); `codex/arms_handbook.json` is the chapter source for the Handbooks codex builder.

`/armsselftest` builds every item stack and checks its components. `tools/gen.py <vanilla assets dir>` regenerates everything under `src/main/resources` plus `library/gear.json`, `codex/` and the two json files; `tools/library_mobs.py <mods dir> <nemesis.json> <out>` writes the mob entries and the merged Library file.

## Transmog table (clean-room replacement for Iron's Lib's transmog table)
`diaithirarms:transmog_table` (Age III, 2 string + 4 planks). Put two armour pieces of the same slot in a crafting grid (works in any grid; the table is the themed place): the first in reading order keeps stats, enchantments and durability and takes the second's worn model and inventory icon. Craft a piece with its own original look to undo it. `diaithirarms:transmog` is the special recipe. Selftest: `/armsselftest` ok=78 bad=0 (75 gear + 3 transmog checks). Not tested: client rendering of a transmogged piece, trims on a transmogged piece. Table texture is generated here; Diaithiris block name pending.

## Armour pack for Immersive Vehicles (clean-room replacement for P:WIC)
This jar also carries an Immersive Vehicles content pack, `assets/diaithirtanks/` (any jar with `assets/<id>/packdefinition.json` in the mods folder is loaded by IV, so no extra jar). Everything is original: `tools/gen_tank.py` builds the OBJ models (boxes, prisms, cylinders), the camouflage textures (forest, desert, winter) and all JSON; `tools/preview_tank.py` renders a software preview (`docs/tank_mk1_preview.png`).
Done: two original tank designs (Mk I with a boxy turret, Mk II low and domed), three marks each (6 vehicles) in forest, desert and winter paint (18 vehicle items), two track parts, diesel engine, crew seat, three guns (105, 120, 125 mm autoloader), armour-piercing and high-explosive shells. IV parses every def without errors and registers all 27 items, checked headless (`summon item ... mts:diaithirtanks.<id>`). Diaithiris names (`names_map_tanks.json`), Age gates (`age_gates_tanks.json`, VII and Rodos), library (`library/tanks_library.json`) and Codex handbook (`codex/tanks_handbook.json`) come from `tools/gen_tank_meta.py`; previews in `docs/`.
Not done: driving, turret turn and firing are untested (no headless spawn harness; the first full-pack client join checks them); tracks are static (no tread path or wheel animation); no instruments; the vehicles are original designs, not copies of the P:WIC set (about 20 variants across an American-pattern and a Soviet-pattern family), so old `mts:pwic_*` ids map to the nearest mark in `id_migrations_tanks.json`.
