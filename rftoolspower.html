This is an unofficial NeoForge 26.1.2 port of RFTools Power by McJty. The original authors did not make it and do not support it, so please report problems with this build at https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/issues and not to them.

What changed

Ported from the 1.21_neo branch. The power cell shows plain stacking models instead of per-side input/output overlays.
