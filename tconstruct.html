This is an unofficial NeoForge 26.1.2 port of Tinkers' Construct by SlimeKnights; 26.1 port by BiAn-CQ. The original authors did not make it and do not support it, so please report problems with this build at https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/issues and not to them.

What changed

Built from BiAn-CQ's community 26.1 port of Tinkers' Construct, plus three small fixes of ours (see the source branch).
