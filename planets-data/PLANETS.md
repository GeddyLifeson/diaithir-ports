# PLANETS: every Panscriptum universe is a planet in the game (Marlon 11-03)

Source: library-content/content.sqlite (217 universes, 699,649 entries) and universe_placement.json. Real data only; nothing invented. Generator: gen_planets.py (data only, no jars). Outputs: planets.tsv, planets.json, datapack/.

## The rule
- **One universe = one planet = one dimension** `diaithir_planets:<planet_id>`. `planet_id` is the lowercase universe name (a-z, 0-9, _), unique across all 217. Diaithir is Terra (the existing Overworld, `minecraft:overworld`); the other 216 are new dimensions.
- **Every entry lives on its planet.** An entry's planet is its `universe` column (id form `REALM.universe.E<era>.collection.number`). NPCs, mobs, bosses, rites, gear, structures, quests and books place by that key, never anywhere else. Tier (Age) gates what appears; a planet opens at the first Age with at least 50 entries (`opens_age`).
- **Planets group into seven soils** (the charter metaverses): Bloom 68, Hollow 42, Storm 35, Frost 26, Iron 25, Ember 16, Quiet 5. The spine code (II.T.1 style) gives the orbit number within its soil.
- **Host terrain per soil** (vanilla dimension types and noise settings, so no new jars): Bloom overworld, Frost amplified, Storm floating islands, Hollow caves, Iron large biomes, Ember nether, Quiet end. Biome palettes, structures and flora come next from each planet's BIOME, DIMENSION, SETTLEMENT and STRUCTURE entries via the biomes-dimensions module.
- **Travel** is by Keelwright space hulls (SHIPBUILDING.md step 9): each soil is a drift region in space, each planet an orbit slot by spine number. Age gate = `opens_age`. Library entries stay readable from the Library dimension.

## For the sibling lanes
Read `planets.tsv` (one row per planet: id, dimension, soil, opens_age, entries and counts per form). Place each entry by its `universe`. Per-planet counts per form are included so you can size your work.

## Biomes and tags (added)
Each non-Terra planet has 4 of its own biomes `diaithir_planets:<planet>_<kind>` (864 total), cloned from vanilla 26.1.2 biome templates by soil (planets-data/templates, from the 26.1.2 vanilla data) with a per-planet colour shift, placed in four temperature/humidity cells in its dimension. The biome tag `#diaithir_planets:<planet_id>` lists exactly that planet's four biomes, so structures keyed on the tag never leak into Terra. Tested: 216 dimensions and 864 biomes load with no registry errors; chunks generated in one planet per soil and in Ashestith, Alaima and Elaennirlys.

## Earth-depth pipeline (Marlon 11-03: every planet as fleshed out as Terra)
Everything is derived from the planet's own Library entries; nothing invented.
1. **Spec (Stage A)**: spec_jobs.py builds one Haiku row per planet (70 sampled BIOME/DIMENSION/SETTLEMENT/STRUCTURE entries, about 7k tokens); spec_run.py runs it (watcher fallback). Output specs/<planet>.json: sky and fog colours, weather, sea liquid (water, lava, none), terrain (normal, amplified, large biomes) and 5-10 biomes, each tied to a real entry id with archetype, climate point and colours.
2. **World (Stage B)**: gen_world.py writes per planet: entry-named biomes (cloned from vanilla 26.1.2 templates, tinted), deep sea, sea, shore, river and two cave biomes (all with its tag `#diaithir_planets:<id>`), its own dimension_type (sky), optional own noise_settings (lava sea), and a multi-noise dimension that mixes them. Terrain, caves, oceans and rivers come from the vanilla overworld noise (or amplified or large biomes). Order: gen_planets.py, then gen_world.py ALL.
3. **Proven on Alaima** (headless 26.1.2): 9 entry-named biomes plus sea, deep sea, shore, river, lush and dripstone caves all generate; sky from the spec.
4. **Other lanes plug in by the tag**: mobs add spawns, structures add sites, NPCs and villages go to settlements, all keyed on `#diaithir_planets:<id>` or the dimension id. Biome names are the real entry names, so a lane can place its entry in the biome whose entry id it shares (`entry` field in specs/<id>.json).
5. **Not yet**: custom trees, plants and ores (needs the pack block mapping via pack_id), eternal day/night clocks, ash and other weather types, villages, per-biome mob lists. Rolled out to the other 215 planets after this is reviewed.
6. **Memory and startup (done)**: dimensions are no longer in the LevelStem registry. Stems live in data/diaithir_planets/planet_stems/<id>.json (with a per-planet neoforge:seed_override, so planets of one soil no longer share terrain). PlanetGate (diaithir-overworld-biomes, Java) creates a planet's ServerLevel the first time it is needed and saves and unloads it after about 40 s empty (no players, no forced chunks). Commands (op): /planet load <id>, /planet go <id>, /planet live. API: PlanetGate.load(server, id), PlanetGate.enter(player, id). Tested headless: boot is vanilla fast with 3 dimensions, all 216 planets load on demand with no errors (about 780 MB RSS with all 216 created and nothing generated), idle planets unload and reload. Known limit: a player who logs out inside a planet is put in the Overworld on next login unless a mod loads that planet first.

## Rollout status (all 216 planets)
Specs for every planet came from one Haiku call each (specs/<id>.json, about 7k tokens in, 1k out). Built: 3,039 entry-named and role biomes, 216 dimension types, 216 planet stems with their own seed. Headless 26.1.2: registry loads clean, boot 5 s, every soil sampled (Ember Brurnadeld, Quiet Kelarnock, Storm Amarnes, plus Alaima and 12 more): land biomes, sea and river locate in generated terrain. Memory: about 100 MB per live generated planet, so size the heap for planets with players; idle ones unload. Caveats: the specs lean on ash weather (142 of 216) and large-biome terrain (139), and ash weather is not implemented yet (rain or snow by biome temperature applies); the Haiku prompt needs a variety nudge before a second pass. A tree/plant/ore feature pass (biomes-dimensions tools) is next.
