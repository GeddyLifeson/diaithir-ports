This is an unofficial NeoForge 26.1.2 port of RFTools Builder by McJty. The original authors did not make it and do not support it, so please report problems with this build at https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/issues and not to them.

What changed

Ported from the 1.21_neo branch. Left out: the Mover module, the 3D shape previews in the shape card, scanner, composer and projector GUIs, the projector's hologram, and the shield's per-projector colour and camouflage (shields show one translucent texture).
