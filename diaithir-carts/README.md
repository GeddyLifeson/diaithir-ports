# Diaithir Carts

Clean-room rail mod for NeoForge 26.1.2, written from public descriptions of the ideas only (faster rail tiers, minecart chains, a cheaper rail tick). No code, art or names from any other mod. Licence: MIT.

- **Rail tiers.** Bronze (Age III), steel (Age V) and high-speed (Age VI) rails, each with a plain and a powered variant. A cart riding one is capped at 0.6, 0.9 or 1.2 blocks per tick (vanilla is 0.4). The plain rails extend the vanilla rail block, so curves, slopes and junctions behave as usual; the powered rails extend the vanilla powered rail (redstone chaining) and boost per tier. Vanilla detector and activator rails work beside them. The cap is lifted by one mixin on `AbstractMinecart.getMaxSpeed`; it only ever raises the cap, and only while the cart is on one of our rails.
- **Coupling chain.** Use it on a cart, then on a second one: the first leads, the second follows at 1.5 blocks and is pulled, so a row of carts is a train (each cart has one leader and one follower; loops are refused). A link breaks past 8 blocks, or when a cart is destroyed, and drops the chain.
- **Chain cutters.** Sneak and use on a cart to cut its link and get the chain back.
- **Station rail.** Stops a cart that rolls onto it and holds it until it is powered or its timer ends (use bare-handed to cycle: signal only, 2, 5, 15, 60 s). Coupled followers follow their leader instead of holding.
- **Block signal.** Faces a rail and guards the stretch of connected track from there (up to 32 rails each way, round curves and slopes). Red with a redstone signal of 15 while a minecart is anywhere on that stretch, green and 0 otherwise; wire it to a station rail or powered rail upstream to keep trains apart. Checks every 10 ticks, no block entity.
- Out of scope: keeping chunks loaded for carts. Not built from the Railcraft idea list: coke oven and blast furnace, tank and loader carts, routing, one-way and locking tracks, signal boxes with distant signals.

`/diaithircarts signaltest` (owners) builds a rail with a corner and a signal beside it, and checks the stretch (25 rails), green with no cart, red with a cart on the far end, the redstone output, and green again. `/diaithircarts selftest` (owners) builds a yard high above spawn and checks the caps of vanilla, bronze, steel and high-speed rails, powered and unpowered powered rails, coupling through the chain logic, follow and spacing, cutting, and both station releases. It reports PASS or what failed.

`tools/gen_assets.py` writes the textures (drawn from scratch), models, blockstates, lang, recipes, loot, tags, `data/diaithircarts/age_gate.json` and `../library-sync/diaithircarts.json`.

## Rail yards, cranes and rolling stock (package net.diaithir.yards)
Shared assembly yard API: `plans/ASSEMBLY_API.md`. This module ships the rail side. `data/rolling_stock.csv` is the sourced roster; `python3 tools/gen_rolling_stock.py` turns it into `yards.json`, bench recipes (`diaithir_stations` format), age gates, models and lang. Four yard controllers (workshop, erecting shop, assembly line, automated), eight cranes (treadwheel to container), parts and 60 vehicles. Use a finished vehicle on a controller to choose that design (it is consumed and rolls out again once built), install parts stage by stage; parts of 500 kg and up need a crane within 8 blocks with enough lift; cranes and higher tiers build faster.
Headless check: `./gradlew runServer -Pyardtest` logs `Yards self test PASS` (recipes, tier lock, crane need, staging, rollout) and stops.
Vehicles: using one on a rail spawns an existing cart (locomotives furnace carts, freight chest or hopper carts, passenger cars plain carts), so coupling, speed tiers and stations apply. Crane operators: a player, or an entity in tag `diaithircarts:crane_operators` (villagers), within 8 blocks of the yard; with a design chosen (the blueprint) and part items in a chest touching the controller, a manned crane installs them stage by stage without hand work.
Not done yet: vehicles are plain carts (no per-class models, mass or power in motion); real textures (placeholders); roster specs (mass, power) are rounded and only the page and year are checked.
## One-way rail, destination rail, cart loader
- **One-way rail**: carts go north or east, or south or west when reversed (bare hand flips it). A cart rolling on against the arrow is turned back. Straight stretches only.
- **Destination rail**: gives redstone 15 while a cart whose name matches its channel stands on it. Channel 0 matches any cart; 1 to 16 are the letters A to P, matched against the cart name (name tag). Bare hand cycles the channel. Power a three-way junction rail with it so named carts take the branch.
- **Cart loader**: put it over a rail with a chest, barrel or tank on top. Every 8 ticks it moves up to 8 items, or 250 mB of fluid, between the container and the cart under it (bare hand flips loading and unloading; redstone switches it off). It uses the item and fluid handlers of the cart, so it works with chest and hopper carts and with the tank and hopper wagons the yards build (wag_tank, wag_open_hopper, wag_covered_hopper); no cart entities are added here, so nothing duplicates the rolling-stock roster.
- Test: `/diaithircarts routingtest` (owners) runs carts against and with one-way rails, named and unnamed carts on a destination rail, and a cart loader loading, unloading and switched off by redstone.
- **Locking rail**: stops a cart and keeps it. Bare hand cycles the mode. Latch (amber) holds until the rail goes from unpowered to powered (a pulse; already powered on arrival does not release). Interlock (green) holds until the 32 rails ahead are clear of other carts. The routing test covers both.
- Coke oven and blast furnace are not built here: they already exist in diaithir-wiretech (ports #163).

## Timetables and autonomy
- **Timetable** (Age V): use on station rails in order to write the stops, then use on a cart to give it the schedule; it loops. A scheduled cart stops only at its next stop and waits there by the station's own rule (a timer, or a redstone signal, so a circuit gate or reader on the station controls release). Sneak and use in the air to wipe it.
- **Crew and autonomy.** A scheduled cart runs only with a living rider aboard (player, villager or other NPC); without one it holds at the next station. Autonomy is never automatic: the vehicle type must be capable (entity type tag `diaithircarts:autonomy_capable`, which other vehicle mods add to) and it must have an **Autonomy Module** (Age VII item, use it on the vehicle) fitted. Then it runs with no crew. `Autonomy.status(vehicle)` gives UNCREWED, CREWED or AUTONOMOUS, and `Autonomy.autonomousCrew(job, vehicle)` is the predicate behind `global.AUTONOMOUS_CREW(job, vehicle)` for the wages thread.
- **Cart reader** (Age V): put it under a rail; it gives a redstone signal for the cart above (0 none, 1 unscheduled, 2 to 13 heading for stop (signal - 2) (13 = stop 11 and later), 14 scheduled but uncrewed, 15 autonomous), so circuit gates can read vehicles. Control goes the other way through the station rail's redstone release.
- Test: `/diaithircarts scheduletest` (owners).

## Rail wagons as cargo carriers
Every freight wagon from the yards is a `CargoCarrier` of the freight layer (entity capability `Freight.CARRIER`, `RailCargo`). The bay is set when the wagon is built, from its type and gauge, and its payload limit is the wagon's rated capacity: flat, container and well wagons take a 20 ft container (4 x 4 x 7 cells; a well wagon is two high), every other standard wagon is a 2 x 2 x 3 loose bay for pallets, barrels, crates and chests, and narrow wagons are 2 wide, so a container never fits (the real reason containers stayed on standard gauge). A player right-clicks a wagon with a load to put it on the aimed column (a container slides in to fit), or sneaks with an empty hand to take the top load off; a loading dock facing a wagon serves everything on it. Loads, containers and their interiors are saved with the cart (`BayTree`). The client draws each load on the wagon (`CargoDraw`, `MinecartCargoRenderMixin`). Wagons stay chest or hopper carts, so cart loaders and hoppers still use the old cart slots; the bay is the freight layer's. Not done: no mass effect on wagon speed yet, containers are one 20 ft per wagon (no 40 ft wagon class), and the drawing has not been seen in a real client.

## Gauges and the full US/UK roster
Two gauges only: standard (1435 mm) and one narrow (3 ft). Design, real figures and the justification are in `docs/GAUGES.md`. Narrow rail (Age III, cheap, 0.5 blocks per tick, tight curves, steep grades, 40% payload, vehicles up to 90 t) and dual-gauge rail (Age V) join the standard bronze, steel and fast rails. A vehicle only moves on its own gauge, dual-gauge rail and gauge-neutral rails (`Gauge`, `GaugeData`, `CartLogic.cap`); on the wrong gauge its cap is 0 and the item will not place. Standard vehicles lose speed on grades and curves, narrow ones much less.
`tools/harvest_locos.py CACHE_DIR` pulls every US and UK locomotive class on Wikipedia (class list from Wikidata, infobox from the article) into `data/locos_raw.csv`, keeping standard gauge and narrow gauge (500 to 1067 mm, metre gauge excluded) and skipping other gauges; `gen_rolling_stock.py` merges them with the curated roster and adds narrow-gauge twins of the wagons and coaches. Locomotives carry a crew count (steam 2, diesel and electric 1) for the wages thread, which charges only while `Autonomy.status` is CREWED. Freight wagons are chest and hopper carts, so the cart loader loads them, and each also has a cargo bay (see Rail wagons as cargo carriers).
Coverage (4,181 vehicles): 4,116 harvested classes (1,324 steam, 831 diesel, 411 electric locomotives; 979 multiple units, railcars and trams; 329 coaches and wagons) plus the curated first roster and narrow twins. Gaps: Wikipedia has no class lists for most wagons and coaches, so those are the categories I could crawl (329) and not every class; mass is missing for 1,606 and power or capacity for 2,032 (estimated by category, rounded); 449 steam classes have no wheel arrangement, 653 no valve gear and 251 no boiler pressure (defaults by size band); 75 diesels no prime mover; the harvest is English Wikipedia only and skips other gauges, metre gauge included; the first roster still holds a few non-US/UK locomotives.

## How each vehicle is built (spec-driven parts)
`tools/harvest_stock.py` (S6902) reads each Wikipedia infobox for wheel arrangement, cylinders, valve gear, boiler pressure, fuel, driver diameter, prime mover, transmission, role and gauge. `gen_rolling_stock.py` turns that into a build chain at the rail yard, and every part is its own slot kind, so two similar classes differ in the parts they need, with no copied recipes:
- **Steam**: frame = lead, driving (by driver diameter) and trailing axles from the Whyte notation; power = boiler by pressure tier (lo under 10 bar, md, hi, ss over 20) and fuel; cylinders = one part per cylinder, by valve gear (Walschaerts, Stephenson, Baker, Caprotti, Southern and others cost different extras); cab; tender, or a water tank for tank engines (shunters); coupler. Made at the wright bench and steam forge.
- **Diesel**: bogies; engine by family and power band plus a transmission (electric, hydraulic or mechanical); cab; coupler. The engine and electric transmission are made at the assembler (machine Age), the rest at the forge and bench.
- **Electric**: bogies; traction motor by power band plus an overhead, third-rail or battery collector.
- **Multiple units, railcars, trams**: bogies, a unit body and either the diesel or the electric power stage.
- **Wagons and coaches**: wheelsets or bogies and a body by type (hopper, tank, box, flat, gondola, reefer, sleeper, dining and so on).
- Narrow gauge uses the light frames, bogies and couplers. The yard controller tier follows the first-service year (workshop, erecting shop, assembly line, automated), so a steam shunter (tank engine, no tender, forge parts) and a diesel shunter (engine and transmission from the assembler) differ in parts, stations and Age.

## Magic fuel (integration matrix)
Every locomotive carries an engine class from the diaithir-units fuel table: `steam_piston`, `diesel_engine`, `electric_motor` (plain string ids, no code dependency; `Fuels.engine(id).accepts(fuelId)` is the check where the units module is present). A Mana Firebox (steam) or Mana Cell (diesel, electric) fitted to a locomotive switches it to `arcane_motor`, which accepts mana, aura, ember, life points, vis and Source (100 J a quantum, Source 1000 J). Magic items in the tag `diaithircarts:magic_fuel` are banked on the vehicle at the magic thread's joules (mana x 100 J: mana powder 4 kJ, pearl 40 kJ, diamond 80 kJ, Source gem 10 kJ), and each fill of fire (a coal's 180 s) costs rated power x 180 s / 0.60. A coal is 24 MJ, so items alone fire only small engines; `MagicFuel.charge(cart, joules)` takes joules from an energy bridge block (1 FE = 1 J). A block that reads the plain energy capability and charges a parked or passing locomotive is not built yet. A purpose-built arcane locomotive class and reading the units module directly are not done.

Textures: `python3 tools/gen_textures.py` draws the 16x16 item and block art (one glyph per class, colour varied by id); run it before `gen_rolling_stock.py`. Roster check: `tools/verify_roster.py` (slow, run from the PC).
