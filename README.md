# Diaithir Compat

On NeoForge's recipe-JSON event it tries every recipe as shipped; if one fails to decode, it upgrades a copy from the 1.21.1 JSON shapes and keeps it only if the copy then decodes. It runs first, so Almost Unified, JAOPCA and KubeJS see 26.1 JSON. It cannot break a working recipe.

It also keeps natural spawns inside geographic ranges on the Tellus Earth dimension. Ranges are datapack files `data/<namespace>/earth_ranges/*.json`: an entity id, an optional species (`species` name or `variant` number, for Untamed Wilds species) and a list of `{"lat": [min, max], "lon": [min, max]}` boxes. When a mob joins the level with the natural or chunk-generation spawn reason, its block position is converted to latitude and longitude with Tellus' own `EarthProjection` at the active preset's world scale, and the spawn is refused outside the boxes. Summons, eggs, spawners, breeding and loaded entities are never touched, entities with no entry are unaffected, and if Tellus is not loaded every spawn is allowed. The shipped files cover Untamed Wilds' mammals, from the regions in the Age I fauna table and today's native ranges (`diaithir-compat/tools/gen_earth_ranges.py` writes them).

It adds wild plants of Age I Earth that no other mod in the pack covers (the gaps are listed in DiaithirRPG `world/earth/age1/FLORA_COVERAGE.csv`): mountain avens, dwarf birch, dwarf willow, steppe sage, joint-fir, crowberry, heather, sea buckthorn, purple saxifrage, Swiss stone pine and wild fig trees (vanilla tree shapes, own leaves and saplings), and wild einkorn, emmer, barley, lentil, pea and chickpea. The cereals and pulses grow only in southwest Asia (the chickpea only in southeast Anatolia) and drop grain or pulses that can be eaten raw. Placement is by vanilla biome through NeoForge biome modifiers, and a placement filter `diaithircompat:earth_region` limits each feature to its `earth_ranges` entry (`flora_<id>.json`) on the Earth dimension; other dimensions are not filtered. `diaithir-compat/tools/gen_flora.py` writes the textures (recoloured from vanilla sprites), models, lang names with scientific names, loot, features and ranges.

It raises the people of Age I sites for the DiaithirRPG city engine (`content/cities/kubejs/server_scripts/cities/cities.js`). When the engine raises a site it runs `/sitepeople spawn <site> <type> <x1> <z1> <x2> <z2> <culture>` with the box of blocks the site placed, and when the site is ruined `/sitepeople remove <site>`. The number of people comes from the site type, and their kit from the culture (`data/diaithircompat/site_people.json`, written by `diaithir-compat/tools/gen_people.py` from DiaithirRPG `world/earth/age1/CULTURE_KITS.csv` and `PEOPLE_SPEC.md`). Each person holds the culture's main-hand item and carries its other items, all Age I things (no metal, no later weapons). Each is a nitwit with no trades, is persistent, and is tagged `diaithir_site` and `site_<key>`. People stand on the lowest open floor in the box, so inside a hut rather than on its roof. A ruined site's people still in unloaded chunks are dropped when their chunk loads (`data/diaithircompat_ruined_sites.txt` in the world). MCA Reborn is an optional dependency, reached by reflection with no compile-time link. When it is loaded, the people are MCA villagers, and their clothing is locked to their kit's dress. Every one of the 74 cultures has its own kit, dressed from `diaithir-compat/tools/culture_dress.csv`: the garment's hide or fibre, its ornament and its pigment, each row with the dated source it comes from and a confidence. The cut follows what the culture wore (fur parka, hide robe or tunic, or wrap and kilt). Each kit has three everyday outfits for men and three for women, plus a ruler's long robe with a bead collar, a warrior's ochre stripes (painted on bare skin where the cut leaves it bare) and a priest's painted robe. Age I had elders and hunters, not kings, so the three roles are design choices. A camp of three or more people keeps one hunter in warrior paint, and diaithir-ages dresses town rulers as rulers and followers as warriors through `SitePeople.dressAs`. The dress is MCA clothing list entries (`data/diaithircompat/skins/clothing/<kit>.json`) with textures recoloured from MCA Reborn's own clothing (`assets/diaithircompat/skins/clothing/normal/<kit>/`). Their profession `diaithircompat:<kit>` belongs to no villager, so MCA never puts them on its own villagers. Without MCA Reborn the people are vanilla villagers of the kit's villager type.

Part of the Diaithir modpack for NeoForge 26.1.2. Source: https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/tree/diaithircompat. Licence: MIT. The site people clothing textures are painted by `diaithir-compat/tools/outfits` (64x, our own art).

# Diaithir Nemesis

A monster that kills a player becomes a nemesis: it gets a name and epithet from the Age's pool, a rank, one trait, and it never despawns. It holds a grudge against that player and their House, and takes the item in the player's hand as a trophy, carried in its off hand and dropped when it falls. The player respawns Shaken (Weakness with no end) until that nemesis falls, and `/nemesis roster` shows where each one was last seen. Each further kill raises its rank (Age I: Prowler, Stalker, Hunter of the Folk, Dread of the Fire, Bane of the Hearths) and adds a trait: hardy, swift, brutal, thick hide, venomous, frostbite, fire thief, bloodfed or caller (brings its kind to an ambush).

A kill is not the only way: damage either way between a monster and one player builds a hidden grudge, shown to that player as it turns resentful (6), vengeful (12) and murderous (20), when the monster becomes a nemesis without a kill. Each nemesis belongs to one of its Age's warbands (Age I: the Frost Pack, the Hollow Ones, the Bone-Gnawers, the Ash Walkers); the highest rank is its chief, and when the chief falls the strongest left rises to lead. Every 30 minutes two nemeses of different warbands clash out of sight: the winner rises, the loser drops a rank or dies and its trophy goes to the winner. Sneak and use on a nemesis below a quarter of its health to interrogate it: it names its chief and where the chief was last seen (lying 20% of the time), then flees. `/nemesis chronicle` lists the last 30 events. Players at the Settler Standing (diaithir-ages `created` and `standing` events) are left out entirely.

Every 5 minutes, each player more than 200 blocks from spawn may be ambushed (25%, at most once per nemesis every 30 minutes) by a nemesis holding a grudge against them or their House. The first time a nemesis dies it may escape with a scar and a new trait (50%). At an Age Turn every nemesis is succeeded by its heir: a new name under the same epithet, one rank lower, half the traits, the same grudges and trophy.

It follows DiaithirRPG's `diaithir_ages` engine by reflection on its `StoryEvent` (Age Turns, House found, join and leave), so neither mod needs the other. Names, ranks, traits and the numbers above are data in `data/diaithirnemesis/nemesis.json`; an Age with no entry uses Age I's. Server-side only: players install nothing.

Commands: `/nemesis roster` (who hunts you), `/nemesis chronicle`; for operators `/nemesis list`, `forget <id>`, `ambush <player>`, `turn <age>`; `/nemesis selftest` (owners, on a world with no nemeses) runs promotion, rank-up, trophy, ambush, escape or fall, save round trip and succession with a fake player and reports PASS or what failed.

Source: https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/tree/diaithircompat/diaithir-nemesis. Licence: MIT.

## diaithir-gear (Gléaus)

Cross-mod gear parity: Point Blank bullet damage scaled against the Musket Mod, shared gem tag with matching Apotheosis and Silent Gems affixes, and one Age-gated set bonus per armour or jewellery family. Data driven, MIT. See `diaithir-gear/README.md` and `diaithir-gear/TEST.md`.

# Diaithir Voice (diaithir-voice)

Proximity voice chat that travels inside the normal Minecraft connection, so a single TCP tunnel (playit.gg free TCP, for one) is enough: no UDP port, no native library. Hold the Push to Talk key (V by default) and players within `range` blocks (32) hear you, at full volume inside `nearDistance` (4) and falling off with the square of the remaining distance, placed left or right by where you stand. The client captures 16 kHz mono with Java Sound, packs 20 ms frames as IMA ADPCM (163 bytes, about 65 kbit/s of payload per speaker), and sends them as a custom payload. The server works the range and gain from the speaker's real position, relays only to listeners in range, rate limits each speaker, and the client plays through a jitter buffer. Both payloads are optional, so vanilla clients and servers still connect.

Latency, honestly: TCP delivers in order and resends lost packets, so a lost packet stalls everything behind it for a round trip or more instead of leaving a gap. Expect the 60 ms jitter buffer, up to 50 ms because the server relays on its tick, plus the network time; on a clean link about 150 to 250 ms mouth to ear, and a bad link shows as short freezes then a skip forward (the buffer is capped at 140 ms and drops the oldest frames rather than fall further behind). A UDP voice mod is lower latency and handles loss better; this one trades that for working through any TCP tunnel. Voice shares the game connection, so about 65 kbit/s per speaker in range goes through it.

Settings: `config/diaithirvoice-common.toml` (range, nearDistance, maxFramesPerSecond) and `config/diaithirvoice-client.toml` (volume, jitterFrames, microphone). The test tone `-Ddiaithir.voice.testtone=440` replaces the microphone. If `diaithirvoiceinteraction` is installed, the server reports each frame's loudness to it. No HUD indicator yet. Licence: MIT. Clean-room: written from how proximity voice behaves, no code from any voice mod.

# Diaithir Voice Interaction (diaithir-voice-interaction)

Creatures of the wild notice speech. A player's voice makes a vibration (a game event of its own, frequency 7) that sculk sensors and wardens hear, and creatures within `noticeRadius` (16 blocks for loud speech, less for quiet) react by rule: wolves, cats, villagers, golems, horses and parrots turn to look, foxes, ocelots, rabbits, chickens and bats take a few steps away. Speaking while sneaking is not noticed. Whispers and private groups are optional. Quiet speech under `thresholdDb` (-45 dB) is ignored, and one speaker is noticed at most every `intervalTicks` (10).

It listens to Simple Voice Chat through its published plugin API (`de.maxhenkel.voicechat:voicechat-api`, compile-time only, nothing bundled; the plugin class is `@ForgeVoicechatPlugin` and only loaded when Simple Voice Chat is) and to diaithir-voice natively through its `net.diaithir.voice.api.SpeechEvent` (build `diaithir-voice` first, it is a compile-time dependency only). One speaker is noticed at most once per `intervalTicks` however many sources report, so Simple Voice Chat and diaithir-voice together do not double trigger. Any other voice source can call `net.diaithir.voiceinteraction.api.VoiceInteractionApi.onSpeech`. The vibration frequency, and which events wardens listen to, are data (`data/neoforge/data_maps/game_event/vibration_frequencies.json`, `data/minecraft/tags/game_event/`) that a datapack can change. Config `config/diaithirvoiceinteraction-common.toml`. Licence: MIT. Clean-room: written from the public description of the voice-interaction addons for Simple Voice Chat, no code, assets or text taken from them.
