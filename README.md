# Diaithir Compat

On NeoForge's recipe-JSON event it tries every recipe as shipped; if one fails to decode, it upgrades a copy from the 1.21.1 JSON shapes and keeps it only if the copy then decodes. It runs first, so Almost Unified, JAOPCA and KubeJS see 26.1 JSON. It cannot break a working recipe.

It also keeps natural spawns inside geographic ranges on the Tellus Earth dimension. Ranges are datapack files `data/<namespace>/earth_ranges/*.json`: an entity id, an optional species (`species` name or `variant` number, for Untamed Wilds species) and a list of `{"lat": [min, max], "lon": [min, max]}` boxes. When a mob joins the level with the natural or chunk-generation spawn reason, its block position is converted to latitude and longitude with Tellus' own `EarthProjection` at the active preset's world scale, and the spawn is refused outside the boxes. Summons, eggs, spawners, breeding and loaded entities are never touched, entities with no entry are unaffected, and if Tellus is not loaded every spawn is allowed. The shipped files cover Untamed Wilds' mammals, from the regions in the Age I fauna table and today's native ranges (`diaithir-compat/tools/gen_earth_ranges.py` writes them).

It adds wild plants of Age I Earth that no other mod in the pack covers (the gaps are listed in DiaithirRPG `world/earth/age1/FLORA_COVERAGE.csv`): mountain avens, dwarf birch, dwarf willow, steppe sage, joint-fir, crowberry, heather, sea buckthorn, purple saxifrage, Swiss stone pine and wild fig trees (vanilla tree shapes, own leaves and saplings), and wild einkorn, emmer, barley, lentil, pea and chickpea. The cereals and pulses grow only in southwest Asia (the chickpea only in southeast Anatolia) and drop grain or pulses that can be eaten raw. Placement is by vanilla biome through NeoForge biome modifiers, and a placement filter `diaithircompat:earth_region` limits each feature to its `earth_ranges` entry (`flora_<id>.json`) on the Earth dimension; other dimensions are not filtered. `diaithir-compat/tools/gen_flora.py` writes the textures (recoloured from vanilla sprites), models, lang names with scientific names, loot, features and ranges.

It raises the people of Age I sites for the DiaithirRPG city engine (`content/cities/kubejs/server_scripts/cities/cities.js`). When the engine raises a site it runs `/sitepeople spawn <site> <type> <x1> <z1> <x2> <z2> <culture>` with the box of blocks the site placed, and when the site is ruined `/sitepeople remove <site>`. The number of people comes from the site type, and their kit from the culture (`data/diaithircompat/site_people.json`, written by `diaithir-compat/tools/gen_people.py` from DiaithirRPG `world/earth/age1/CULTURE_KITS.csv` and `PEOPLE_SPEC.md`). Each person holds the culture's main-hand item and carries its other items, all Age I things (no metal, no later weapons). Each is a nitwit with no trades, is persistent, and is tagged `diaithir_site` and `site_<key>`. People stand on the lowest open floor in the box, so inside a hut rather than on its roof. A ruined site's people still in unloaded chunks are dropped when their chunk loads (`data/diaithircompat_ruined_sites.txt` in the world). MCA Reborn is an optional dependency, reached by reflection with no compile-time link. When it is loaded, the people are MCA villagers, and their clothing is locked to their kit's dress. That dress is MCA clothing list entries (`data/diaithircompat/skins/clothing/<kit>.json`) with textures recoloured from MCA Reborn's own clothing in the kit's three colours (`assets/diaithircompat/skins/clothing/normal/<kit>/`). Their profession `diaithircompat:<kit>` belongs to no villager, so MCA never puts them on its own villagers. Without MCA Reborn the people are vanilla villagers of the kit's villager type.
