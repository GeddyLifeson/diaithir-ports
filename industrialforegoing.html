This is an unofficial NeoForge 26.1.2 port of Industrial Foregoing by Buuz135 and InnovativeOnlineIndustries. The original authors did not make it and do not support it, so please report problems with this build at https://github.com/GeddyLifeson/diaithir-ports/issues and not to them.

What changed

Ported from 1.21.1 onto Titanium's official 26.1.2 release. Infinity tools keep charge and fluids through the caller's transaction, so chargers and pipes no longer lose energy. Left out: contributor cosmetics (they downloaded a player list), the hydroponic processor's Shift toggle, spawn-egg colouring on the mob imprisonment tool, the Enchantment Applicator's enchantability check. Old saves do not carry over.
